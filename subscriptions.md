---
title: Subscriptions
---

Mortar shows subscription options when a product has **selling plans** from a subscription app, such as Shopify Subscriptions. Mortar doesn't create subscriptions itself.

- **Buy buttons block:** customers choose between a one-time purchase and each subscription plan. The price, savings and price per delivery update with the plan. Turn on **Select subscription by default** to make subscribing the first choice.
- **Cart and orders:** the plan name shows under each subscribed item.
- **Subscription promo section:** explain how your subscription works in three steps and feature a product that has selling plans.
