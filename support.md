title: Support
---

We help with theme setup, theme settings and bugs in Mortar's code. We reply within one business day.

**Covered:** questions about settings, sections and blocks; problems caused by Mortar's code; help choosing settings for your use case.
**Not covered:** custom code changes, issues caused by third-party apps or edited theme code, store setup outside the theme (payments, shipping, domains).

**[Open the support form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd_2y2vJaiQrAsf0tdktcxgTiGg_ao5CkzJ8O6g4Rdlp1QPkw/viewform)** - tell us your name, email, store URL (for example `yourstore.myshopify.com`), the preset you use, and what happened. Attach screenshots in the form. You get an automatic confirmation email.

Before customizing code, duplicate your theme. For larger changes, consider hiring a Shopify Partner.
