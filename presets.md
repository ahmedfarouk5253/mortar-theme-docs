title: Presets
---

| Preset | Made for | Look | Highlights |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mortar** | Pantry brands and specialty grocers | Warm paper and olive colors, Fraunces and DM Sans, arched images, tag-shaped product cards | Label band on cards, nutrition and allergens on every product, recipe posts, right-to-left ready |
| **Crema** | Coffee roasters | Dark espresso and bone colors, Space Grotesk and Inter, square images | Subscription-first buying, tasting flight, origin facts, brew gear lookbook |
| **Crumb** | Bakeries and patisseries | Butter and raspberry colors, Playfair Display and Nunito Sans, scalloped images, pill buttons | Pickup banner, preferred date in the cart, allergens up front, menu-style shelf |

You can switch presets in **Theme settings > Theme style**. Switching changes colors, fonts, shapes and cards; it doesn't change your sections.
