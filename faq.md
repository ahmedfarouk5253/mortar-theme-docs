---
title: FAQ
---

- **Why doesn't my nutrition table show?** It needs at least one row in the format `Name | value | value`. Check the block setting or the connected metafield.
- **Why don't I see the country selector?** It shows when your store sells to more than one country (Settings > Markets). The language selector shows when you've published more than one language.
- **Can I use my own fonts?** Mortar uses Shopify's font library so your store stays fast and licensed. Custom font files aren't supported.
- **Why are there no countdown timers or "only 2 left" messages?** Mortar shows only real information. The Inventory status block uses your actual stock and a threshold you choose.
- **How do I add a discount code field?** Customers enter discount codes at checkout. Automatic discounts show in the cart.
- **Does Mortar include reviews or a wishlist?** No. Add them with an app; the product page and sections accept app blocks.
- **How do I hide the "Powered by Shopify" link?** Online Store > Themes > ⋯ > Edit default theme content > search "Powered by".
