---
title: Right-to-left languages
---

Mortar is fully bidirectional. When a customer views your store in Arabic, Hebrew, Persian or Urdu, the whole layout mirrors: menus, drawers, sliders, arrows, the cart and the product gallery.

- **Preview:** publish the language in Settings > Languages, then open `yourstore.com/ar` (or `/he`). The theme editor previews your store's default language.
- **Fonts:** Shopify's font library includes Latin characters only. Mortar uses your chosen fonts for Latin text and falls back to high-quality system fonts for Arabic and Hebrew.
- **Arabic typography:** Mortar removes letter spacing, uppercase styling and italics for Arabic script and adds a little extra line height so text stays readable.
- **Numbers, prices and codes** (SKUs, phone numbers, gift card codes) stay left-to-right inside right-to-left text, so they read correctly.
- **Lookbook hotspots** keep their position on the image in every language, because the image itself doesn't mirror.
