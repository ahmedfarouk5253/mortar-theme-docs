All notable changes to Mortar are documented here. This project follows semantic versioning.

### 1.0.0 — First release

Mortar is a theme for artisan food and drink makers, with three presets: Mortar (pantry), Crema (coffee) and Crumb (bakery).

#### Highlights
- Product pages built for food: profile meters, origin facts, nutrition table, allergens, unit prices, subscribe or one-time choice for products with selling plans, and an inventory status that uses real stock.
- Local pickup and a preferred date field in the cart, with an optional order cutoff message.
- Recipe articles with ingredients, method and Recipe structured data.
- Header with dropdown or mega menu (up to three levels, and up to two promos per mega menu item), announcement bar, predictive search, country and language selectors.
- Full right-to-left support, tested with Arabic.
- Color schemes that pass contrast checks, and Lighthouse accessibility of 100 on the demo stores.

#### Changed
- The coffee preset is named Crema (it was Roast during development).
- Theme information now links to the published documentation and support form.
