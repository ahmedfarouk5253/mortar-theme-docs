---
title: Updating the theme
---

1. Online Store > Themes > ⋯ > **Duplicate** your live theme first.
2. Add the new version from the Theme Store. Shopify copies your settings and content to it.
3. Preview the new version, check your custom code if you have any, then publish.

If you edit theme code, your changes aren't carried over automatically. Keep a note of them, or hire a Shopify Partner for larger customizations.
