---
title: Local pickup and preferred date
---

**Pickup availability** (product page block): shows whether the selected variant can be picked up at your nearest pickup location and how soon it's usually ready, with a drawer listing every location. Set up pickup in Settings > Shipping and delivery > Local pickup. The block hides when no location offers pickup.

**Preferred date** (Theme settings > Cart > Show preferred date field): adds a date field to the cart. The date is saved with the order as a cart attribute named "Preferred date". It doesn't check your availability or capacity; it's a request customers make, and you confirm it.

**Order cutoff message** (Theme settings > Cart): a short message such as "Order by 6pm for next-day pickup". It's static text, never a countdown.

**Pickup banner section:** shows your address, opening hours and a static map image or shop photo. No map embed or location tracking is loaded.
