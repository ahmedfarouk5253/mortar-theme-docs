## Colors

Color schemes are sets of colors that sections, blocks and the header and footer can use. Each scheme has:

- **Background**
- **Background gradient** — Replaces the solid background color when set.
- **Text**
- **Heading**
- **Button**
- **Button label**
- **Secondary button border**
- **Muted background**
- **Muted text**
- **Border**
- **Sale badge**
- **Sale badge text**

Every preset includes five schemes that pass WCAG AA contrast. If you change colors, keep text at a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background.
