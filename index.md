Mortar is a Shopify theme for food and drink makers: coffee roasters, bakeries and pantry brands. It helps you sell by showing what's inside each product (origin, ingredients, tasting notes, unit price) and how customers can get it (subscribe, pick up or ship).

## Getting started

1. **Install a preset.** In the Shopify Theme Store, choose Mortar (pantry), Crema (coffee) or Crumb (bakery). You can change colors, fonts and layout later; the preset is only a starting point.
2. **Add your logo and colors.** Theme settings > Logo and favicon, then Theme settings > Colors. Each preset has five color schemes that pass accessibility contrast checks.
3. **Set up menus.** Content > Menus. The header uses the `main-menu` menu (up to three levels) and the footer uses `footer`.
4. **Create product metafields** for origin, tasting notes, nutrition and allergens, and connect them to the product page blocks. See [Food blocks and metafields](food-blocks-and-metafields.html).
5. **Fill the home page.** Every section starts with text that explains what to put there. Replace it with your own words and images.
6. **Preview on mobile and in every language you sell in.** If you sell in Arabic or Hebrew, preview the `/ar` or `/he` version of your store.
