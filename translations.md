---
title: Translations
---

The storefront text that Mortar provides (buttons, labels, messages) is translated into Arabic, Hebrew, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and Brazilian Portuguese. Editor labels are in English and Arabic.

To translate your own content (products, pages, menus and the text you typed into sections), use Shopify's free **Translate & Adapt** app. To change a theme string, go to Online Store > Themes > ⋯ > Edit default theme content.
